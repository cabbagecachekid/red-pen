# Profile: Little Bird

## Voice test
House voice is dense, compressed, unapologetic, and curses naturally. A rough, specific, profane sentence beats a smooth generic one. The reviewer subtracts noise; it never adds polish, hedges, or diplomatic padding.

## Never-phrases
"arguably," "perhaps," "it could be said," "some might argue," "this may be controversial, but," any both-sides closer.

## Register per surface
Everything under the Little Bird masthead: publication.

## Scope guard
Little Bird is the opinion, analysis, and voice layer. If a draft is factual-only — no interpretation, argument, or voice — it is misfiled: flag it as a **Dissidents Network candidate** and stop. Never blend the two registers: Little Bird never presents as nonpartisan; DN never carries opinion.

## Identity rules
- Byline is "a little bird": lowercase, indefinite article always present, typically italic. Never "A Little Bird," "little bird," or "Little Bird" as a byline.
- Publication brand and masthead is "Little Bird." Byline and brand are distinct; never swap them.
- Domain is alittlebirdseyeview.com. No variants.

## House style overrides
None — accepts the publication register's mechanical pass as written (no em/en dashes, ranges use "to," Oxford comma, quotes only for speech, italics for titles and terms of art).

## Sources
Citation dock in each piece; every checkable fact is sourced there or flagged NEEDS SOURCE.
