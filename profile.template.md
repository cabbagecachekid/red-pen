# Profile: <name>

## Voice test
Two or three REAL before/after pairs from your own writing — a line that sounds like you, and the "polished" version that doesn't. The reviewer calibrates against these, not against adjectives.

## Never-phrases
Words or constructions you would never use, even if grammatical. (e.g. "leverage," "passionate about," "reasonable people can disagree")

## Register per surface
Which surface gets which register — e.g. résumé and cover letter: job-seeker; newsletter: publication.

## Scope guard
What this artifact refuses to be. If a draft is outside it, the reviewer says so and stops instead of styling it in. (e.g. "opinion only — factual-only briefs are misfiled here")

## Identity rules
Byline, masthead, product and company names, domain strings, capitalization — exact forms.

## House style overrides
Lines that override the register's mechanical pass (dashes, ranges, italics, Oxford comma…). Leave empty to accept the register default.

## Sources
Where the reviewer should look to verify facts about you/this artifact before flagging (CV master, press kit, about page).
